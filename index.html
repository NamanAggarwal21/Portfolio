Lead story · About my work

I build backend and AI systems that keep travellers moving

Support platforms, AI agents and APIs for ixigo, ConfirmTkt and AbhiBus, serving hundreds of thousands of requests a day.

Most travellers who open a support chat on ixigo, ConfirmTkt or AbhiBus now talk first to TARA OS, the AI-first chat platform where I engineered the core features.

It handles 33K+ chats a day, and the LLM workflows I integrated classify tickets and read customer intent along the way. Only about 21% of chats ever reach a person.

When one does, the agent works in OneView, the support console I re-architected on GraphQL. More in Work →
